This is a little bit of information, and I hope you enjoy learning more about them!

Enhypen is a South Korean boy group that was formed through the survival show known as ILAND, directed by Jeong Min-seok and and Kim Shin-young, in 2020. It originally contained seven members, namely Lee Heeseung, Park Jongseong, Sim Jaehyun, Park Sunghoon, Kim Sunoo, Yang Jungwon, and Nishimura Riki.

The group is under BeLift Lab, a sector of Hybe Entertainment, and now functions as a six-member fully fledged unit, working to gain fans internationally as well as with traditional South Korean roots.

The chosen name, Enhypen, represents the en dash and the hyphen that connects people and allows them to discover others, which is the main goal of the group.

Some notable awards and achievements they have received include winning the Artist of the Year as well as the Album of the Year in 2026 at the 2026 Asia Star Entertainer Awards. They have brought home 24 first place trophies from various music shows, with notable wins for their latest tracks "Bloody Paradise" and "Knife".

The group continues to evolve and grow, captivating audiences with their unique sound and dynamic performances. Their vampire-themed performances have captivated the hearts of many young fans, and their fandom, collectively known as ENGENE, continues to support them in their journey towards success.
